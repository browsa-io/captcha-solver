Privacy

The extension talks to one service, Browsa's captcha API, and only in these cases.

When it sends something

  • Solving a captcha. reCAPTCHA, hCaptcha and DataDome start by themselves only in the provider's own frame (checked by its address); GeeTest and AWS WAF only when the page loaded the provider's own scripts; picture-text captchas only on sites where you said yes, otherwise when you press Solve.
  • At most 3 automatic solves per page and 10 per site per hour. A picture already solved is never sent again.
  • Checking your key, when you open the panel or change the key: the key only.

What a solve sends

  • the captcha's own picture(s) and its question (for example "traffic lights"). When a picture can only be read from the screen (hCaptcha, or a picture a page script cannot read), a crop of just the captcha is sent; the rest of the screenshot never leaves the browser;
  • the host name of the site the captcha is on (for example example.com), never the full address;
  • your Browsa API key, so the solve is counted for your workspace.

What it never sends: cookies, the page's HTML or text, form contents, passwords, your browsing history, or anything from pages without a supported captcha. Cloudflare's checks, Altcha and Press & hold are solved in the page: nothing is sent for them.

What it fetches: GeeTest's own puzzle pictures, from GeeTest's servers, the same files the page already loaded.

What is kept

Browsa does not store captcha pictures: a solve keeps its type, the site's host, sizes, timing and model cost, and its answer is deleted after 10 minutes. In your browser: settings, your key and its last check, the sites you allowed for picture text, and the last 50 solves (time, site, type, result, reason); until the browser closes, the solve counters and, for 30 minutes, the answers of pictures already solved.

"Trusted input" (off unless you turn it on) lets the extension send pointer and key events through Chrome's debugger interface; Chrome shows a bar while it is attached. Nothing else is read through it.

Privacy

The extension talks to one service, Browsa's captcha API, and only in these cases.

When it sends something

  • Solving a captcha. reCAPTCHA (pictures and its audio challenge), hCaptcha, MTCaptcha and DataDome start by themselves only in the provider's own frame (checked by its address); GeeTest, AWS WAF, Tencent, NetEase Yidun, Dingxiang, Cutcaptcha and CyberSiARA only when the page loaded the provider's own scripts; picture-text captchas only on sites where you said yes or marked the picture yourself, otherwise when you press Solve.
  • At most 3 automatic solves per page and 10 per site per hour. A picture already solved is never sent again. Never on a site you skip, outside your allow list, or for a type you switched off.
  • Checking your key, when you open the panel, change the key or after a paid solve: the key only.
  • Reporting a wrong answer, only when you press "That was wrong": your key and that answer's id.

What a solve sends

  • the captcha's own picture(s) and its question (for example "traffic lights"); for a puzzle, what it shows and where its parts sit (the tiles' pictures and sizes, the reference icons; for GeeTest's IconCrush and gobang only which icon is on which tile); for reCAPTCHA's audio challenge (used only when picture rounds keep failing and its switch is on), Google's sound clip. When a picture can only be read from the screen (hCaptcha, or a picture a page script cannot read), a crop of just the captcha is sent; the rest of the screenshot never leaves the browser;
  • the host name of the site the captcha is on (for example example.com), never the full address;
  • your Browsa API key, so the solve is counted for your workspace.

What it never sends: cookies, the page's HTML or text, form contents, passwords, your browsing history, or anything from pages without a supported captcha. ALTCHA, Cloudflare's checks and Press & hold are handled in the page: nothing is sent for them. No analytics, no error reporting, no remote code.

What it fetches: a captcha's own puzzle files, from that provider's own servers only, the same files the page already loaded (GeeTest's, Tencent's, NetEase Yidun's and Dingxiang's pictures, CyberSiARA's animation). On install it opens the welcome page, browsa.io/extension.

What is kept

Browsa does not store captcha pictures: a solve keeps its type, the site's host, sizes, timing and model cost, and its answer is deleted after 10 minutes. In your browser, on this device: settings, your key and its last check, the sites you allowed for picture text, the pictures you marked, and the last 50 solves (time, site, type, result, reason, what was charged); until the browser closes, the solve counters and, for 30 minutes, the answers of pictures already solved. Export writes your settings to a file you choose, without your key unless you ask.

What it may do, and why

All sitesA captcha can be on any site, inside a frame from its provider. The extension looks for one, reads only its picture and acts only on it.
StorageSettings, your key and the last 50 solves, on this device.
Right-click menuThe two "this picture is a captcha" entries.
Debugger (Chrome, only if you turn on Trusted input)Real pointer and key events for sites that refuse page events. Chrome shows a bar while it is attached. Nothing is read through it.
